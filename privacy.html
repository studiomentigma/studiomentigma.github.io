1. Introduction

Welcome to Waterway Blocks, a mobile puzzle game developed by Mentigma Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Waterway Blocks. By downloading or using the game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not directly ask for your name, email address, phone number, or home address to play. However, the game uses third-party services that may automatically collect the following information:

2.1 Device and Advertising Information

Our advertising provider, Google AdMob, may collect or process:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, and similar technical data)
  • Information about ad interactions and ad performance (impressions, clicks, and similar events)

2.2 App Diagnostics

The game and its SDKs (including Unity and Google services) may collect crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostic information to help us find and fix technical problems.

2.3 Purchase Information

Waterway Blocks may offer optional one-time in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Google Play handles your payment details, and we do not receive or store your payment card information. We may receive purchase confirmation data (such as product ID and purchase status) so we can unlock the items you bought and restore them on request.

2.4 Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress, settings, and purchase status may be stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising

Waterway Blocks uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, which may include:

  • Interstitial advertisements displayed between game levels
  • Rewarded advertisements that give you optional in-game rewards, such as hints, lives, tokens, or extra time

AdMob may use advertising identifiers and other information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve ads, subject to your settings and applicable law.

Your advertising choices: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

More information:

  • How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google ad controls

4. How We Use Information

Information processed through third-party services is used for the following purposes:

  • To display and deliver advertisements
  • To measure advertising performance and for analytics
  • To process and restore in-app purchases
  • To prevent fraud and abuse
  • To maintain, debug, and improve game performance

Mentigma Studio does not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with or collected by third-party service providers that operate inside the game, as described below:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (advertising and measurement)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)
  • Unity Technologies services included in the game engine (diagnostics, and in-app purchasing support)

These providers process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the game.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their own retention practices. We do not operate our own servers that store personal user data. Locally stored game data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

You can contact us at studiomentigma@gmail.com with questions about data processing or deletion requests, and we will take reasonable steps to respond.

7. Children's Privacy

Waterway Blocks is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us at studiomentigma@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by our services. Data sent to our partners' services is transmitted over encrypted connections where supported. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. This may include rights under the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), and under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) for users in Türkiye.

Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled by the relevant service provider (for example, through your Google Account settings). You may also contact us at studiomentigma@gmail.com and we will help direct your request.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the game, third-party services, or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
